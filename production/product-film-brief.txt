VISUAL / MOTION GRAPHICS RULES
================================================

1. PEOPLE ARE NOT THE HERO.

Use people only when necessary for context.

The HERO should always be:

- UI
- 3D food
- QR
- AR
- Data
- Motion graphics
- Product ecosystem


2. EVERY SCENE SHOULD TRANSFORM INTO THE NEXT.

Avoid:

Scene ends
→
random cut
→
new scene.

Prefer:

Menu
→
PDF
→
phone
→
QR
→
HoloMenu
→
UI
→
3D
→
AR
→
Order
→
Tracking
→
Dashboard
→
Growth.


3. USE MORPHING TRANSITIONS.

Examples:

QR → Logo

Food image → 3D model

3D → AR

AR → Order

Order → Tracking

Tracking → Kitchen

Kitchen → Dashboard

Dashboard → Ecosystem

Ecosystem → Growth


4. CAMERA LANGUAGE

Use:

Slow push-ins
Smooth orbital camera
Macro close-ups
Controlled dolly
Parallax
Depth-of-field
Elegant 3D rotations
Macro-to-wide transitions

Avoid:

Handheld camera
Random zooms
Excessive camera shake
Fast TikTok-style cuts
Generic AI camera movement


5. UI ANIMATION

Everything should feel physically connected.

Buttons:

Fade + slight upward movement

Cards:

Slide + scale

Charts:

Draw progressively

Numbers:

Count upward

Icons:

Scale + opacity

Transitions:

Morph / dissolve / particle


6. FOOD ANIMATION

Food must remain photorealistic.

Use:

Slow rotation
Ingredient separation
Ingredient orbit
Macro camera
Controlled lighting
Subtle steam
Realistic reflections
Realistic shadows

Avoid floating food that looks artificial.


7. TYPOGRAPHY

Use large typography as a visual element.

Examples:

A MENU
SHOULD DO
MORE.

FRICTION.

JUST SCAN.

EXPLORE.

3D.

AR.

ORDER.

TRACK.

GROW.

MAKE YOUR
MENU AN
EXPERIENCE.


8. BRAND FEEL

Premium.
Minimal.
Technological.
Restaurant-focused.
Not overly futuristic.
Not cyberpunk.
Not cartoon.
Not generic SaaS.

Think:

PREMIUM RESTAURANT
+
APPLE-STYLE PRODUCT FILM
+
MODERN 3D MOTION DESIGN
+
